# Artopia – Art and Recreation Website

Artopia is a web-based platform designed to bring students together through art and recreational activities. It provides a centralized place where students can explore activities, discover upcoming events, view multimedia content, and connect with others who share similar interests.

## About the Project

Artopia was developed as a group assignment for the Fundamental of Web Design & Development module at Techspire College, APU.

The project aims to encourage students to participate in creative and recreational activities while providing an engaging and user-friendly online experience.

## Features

- **Home:** Introduction to Artopia with featured activities and events.
- **About:** Information about the website's purpose, mission, and vision.
- **Events:** Information about upcoming and past events, activities, and workshops.
- **Gallery:** Photos and videos showcasing activities and events.
- **Activities:** Information about camping, hiking, dancing, painting, and rock climbing.
- **Members:** A section showcasing community members and participants.
- **Join:** A membership form for students interested in joining Artopia.
- **Resources:** Guides, tips, and safety information for recreational activities.
- **Contact:** Contact information and an inquiry form.
- **FAQs:** Answers to common questions about activities, events, and membership.

## Technologies Used

- HTML5 – Website structure and content
- CSS3 – Styling and responsive layouts
- JavaScript – Interactive features and form validation

## Project Objectives

- Provide a centralized platform for art and recreational activities.
- Make it easier for students to discover events and workshops.
- Encourage student participation and social interaction.
- Provide an engaging experience through multimedia and interactive features.
- Create a responsive website accessible across different devices and browsers.
